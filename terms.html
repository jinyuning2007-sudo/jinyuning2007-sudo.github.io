Terms of use

Personal use

This utility is intended for its operator to back up data they own or are authorized to store. It is not offered as a public backup service. This website provides application information and does not provide access to the operator’s files or computer.

Account and recovery responsibilities

The operator controls the destination Google account, storage capacity, authorization and encryption password. They are responsible for retaining the password securely, checking backup results and maintaining suitable independent recovery copies. Uploads may pause or fail because of connectivity, storage capacity, authorization or source-file changes.

Verification and availability

The utility uses integrity checks and restore tests to assess backup results. These checks do not guarantee uninterrupted operation or recovery from every possible failure. A backup is complete only when its recorded verification has succeeded.

Third-party software and services

Immich, Restic and rclone are independent open-source projects; their respective licenses apply. Google Drive and the website host operate under their own terms. This utility is not endorsed by those projects or by Google.

Privacy and support

Data practices are described in the privacy policy. Contact the operator using the support address displayed on Google’s authorization screen.

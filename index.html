Personal backup utility

Encrypted recovery copies,
stored in the owner’s Drive.

Immich Encrypted Backup is a private utility for backing up the operator’s own photos, videos, companion files and recovery metadata to the operator’s Google Drive account.

Encrypt on the computer

Restic encrypts backup contents, filenames and recovery metadata before upload. The encryption password stays on the operator’s computer.

Upload changes

Restic and rclone upload new or changed data and reuse previously stored content. Recovery points are kept until the operator chooses to remove them.

Limit Drive access

The utility requests Google’s drive.file permission to manage the backup files and folders it creates. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts or calendars.

About this website

These pages describe the utility and its data practices for Google authorization. They provide no access to backups, media, credentials or server administration. The utility is for personal use and is not a public storage service or an official Immich or Google product.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use before authorizing the utility.

Operator and support

The application is operated by the Google account owner who authorizes it. For support, use the application’s support contact displayed on Google’s authorization screen.
